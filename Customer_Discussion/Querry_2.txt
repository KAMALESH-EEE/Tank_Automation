Q_2_1:Power supply architecture

Answer for Qurry Q_2_1:

    The supply is designed to operate upto 28VDC.
    TVS diode is to supress the spikes above 28V
    C4, TR1, C1, C3 are act as EMI filter.
    EMI filter output ~24V used operate relay for water pump, and used to generate 5V.
    U1, U2 are 5V, 1A LDO's parllel operation will be improve the thermal performance.


Q_2_2: HC-SR04 interface
Answer for Qurry Q_2_2:

    J2 connector is interface for HC-SR04
    LM348, U3:B, ECHO signal from HC-SR04 is 5V level, will step down to 3.3V using voltage divider circuit, and the LM348 act as buffer
    LM348, U3:C, TRIG signal from PICO is 3.3V level, will step up to 5V using LM348 as well as act as buffer

Q_2_3: LM348 selection
Answer for Qurry Q_2_3:    LM348 is a 4 op-amp IC, 3 will be used in design for buffer and level shifter.

Q_2_4: Pump switching interface
Answer for Qurry Q_2_4:

    Coil Voltage        :   5VDC

    Coil Current        :   72mA
    *D3 will be used act as free wheel diode
    *3.3V will step up to 5V
